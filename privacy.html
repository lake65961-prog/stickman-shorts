Stickman Shorts: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Stickman Shorts is used only by its owner. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from visitors or any other users.

When the owner connects their TikTok account, an access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is used only to upload the owner's own videos through TikTok's official API and is never sent anywhere else.

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings.
